PILOT SURVIVAL & COMBAT PROTOCOLS

OVERCLOCK is a high-octane 2D top-down cyberpunk mech shooter designed for instant LAN multiplayer and intense tactical combat.

🎯 CORE COMBAT LOOP

Move & Aim: Steer your mech with WASD and trackpad/mouse crosshair. Firing can be done via Left Click or keyboard J.

Twin Shields & Hull: Kinetic shields absorb damage first. When shields shatter, damage hits your armor hull. Shields begin recharging automatically after 3 seconds of taking no damage.

Thruster Dash: Tap Space, Shift, or K for an invincible burst of speed to slip out of explosive radius or pierce through enemy fire.

🛡️ ADVANCED COMBAT TACTICS

Deflect / Parry Shield (C / V): Raise a 0.38s kinetic deflection field. Any projectile hitting your shield during this window is reflected back at the attacker with +35% speed and damage!

Safe Rocket Jump: Fire micro-missiles directly into nearby walls or feet. Explosive weapons deal 0 self-damage to pilots, but provide massive blast propulsion to escape corners!

Super Meter (100%): Scoring hits and taking damage fills your quantum core. Press Q / F / L when fully charged to unleash your chassis signature ultimate!

DEATHMATCH
💀 FREE FOR ALL (SOLO ARENA)

Every pilot fights for themselves in an unrestricted battleground. Score eliminations, grab supply crates, and reach 20 frags (or hold highest kills when the clock expires) to secure MVP victory.

TEAM PLAY
⚔️ TEAM DEATHMATCH (BLUE VS RED)

Pilots are divided into Blue and Red factions. Coordinate flanks, defend power weapon spawn zones, and combine super abilities to outscore the opposing squad.
